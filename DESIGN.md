# FOLDFIELD — places made of possibility

26 September 2026. Original fictional paper architecture studio; sole-agent production and self-review. Active project 02 of thirteen.

## Identity and composition

Three oversized postcards become small places for reading in rain, listening to wind and watching an evening performance. The remembered interaction is a flat, printed cut pattern becoming a room, then folding back along the same hinges. A useful configuration brief is the destination.

Art direction: an experimental paper laboratory. Ultramarine ink, persimmon card, citron accents and warm white, with aluminium hinge pins and translucent vellum. Barlow Condensed ExtraBold gives oversized compressed typography; Space Grotesk supplies readable copy; IBM Plex Mono labels dimensions. Self-host the verified OFL sources. No coastal motifs, rounded hospitality UI or serif-led travel-poster composition from project 01.

## Mechanical and material system

Rain Library: one scored back wall hinged to the card, a roof hinged to its top edge, two side screens hinged along the base, and a linked four-leg folding seat. The wall clears first (first 45% of tab travel); the roof then rotates from the wall's continuation into an upward sloping canopy (50–100%). Closing reverses this dependency. The flat roof and wall occupy consecutive card areas, not the same plane. Side screens unfold outside the footprint. Model thickness, offset pin axes and cut clearances explicitly.

Listening Pavilion: three parallel aluminium/paper portals rise from distinct strips of the card. Inset hanging sails fit within the portals when flat and sway only once raised. The three portals make a rhythm around a quiet seat; they do not reuse the Rain Library silhouette. A wind control changes the sails' movement.

Sunset Theatre: four separated rear wings and hinged canopy strips form a warm, splayed stage, with folded risers and small audience benches. Low sun projects distinct striped shadows. Canopy movement comes from attached pivots with enough clearance to close. Its colour and fan silhouette differ from the Library and Pavilion.

Original surfaces: seeded paper fibre/grain with small embossed scoring, physically thin sheet edges, folded gutters, brushed pins, printed registration marks, cut/crease symbols and measured plan graphics. Use actual geometry for folded edges and props, procedural texture for fibres, selective translucency for vellum. A diffuse environment and raking key reveal thickness and contact. Avoid glossy plastic paper. One movable sun must cast actual shadows.

## Journey and rules

Routes: `/` study archive; `/studio` selected study and interactive tools; `/brief` finished local specification; `/about` scope/process; not-found view. Selection/configuration is validated in a shareable query and optional local save. Back/forward and direct loads preserve it. Essential copy and navigation render into initial HTML.

Choose a purpose/study, unfold, adapt, inspect plan/section/inhabited views, then produce a brief. The Rain Library is the deep configurator: 2.4–4.8 m span, 8–32 degree roof rise and open/slatted/vellum side screens. These change actual roof width/pitch/panels and the same plan/section geometry. The ray-based rain study samples the reading seat and checks roof/wall/screen intersections; it is an authored geometric demonstration, not engineering certification. Side-view aperture is measured using a second set of rays. Drawing, scene, metrics and brief use one configuration.

Optional challenge: keep at least 96% of the marked seat dry under the shown oblique shower and preserve at least two thirds of the side view. No opaque creativity score. Show both conditions separately, reveal useful progressive hints, allow reset and accept multiple geometric solutions. The challenge never gates the brief. Completion yields a stamped card and replay.

Listening and Theatre have bounded controls appropriate to their purposes: wind/sail response or sun/canopy light. They still unfold, close, expose useful views and produce a brief with their real choices. Do not imply equally deep structural configurators for all three.

The final brief states purpose, dimensions, roof/panel choice or study controls, materials, visual plan/section, shelter observations and the fictional/non-engineering scope. Print and locally download a postcard. No submission, contact details, real architecture commission or price quotation.

## Motion and sound

Tab movement owns a single 0–1 fold scalar. GSAP eases toward pointer/range/button targets; each panel's transform is derived from this scalar so rapid reversal cannot break the order. Card selection folds away before the next study resolves. Camera changes use composed views. The fold range can scrub intermediate states; buttons/keyboard provide equivalents to dragging. Prototype mode reveals crease/pin construction. Pausing stops rain/wind decoration; meaningful changes still work. Reduced motion cuts to requested states.

Paper clicks and catch tones may be generated locally after explicit sound opt-in; no sound is required for understanding. If included, document control tests separately from any actual listening review. No autoplay or borrowed track.
