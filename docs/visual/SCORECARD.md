No hero average reaches 4; materials are below 3 throughout. The release bar fails. The pure inspection camera cropping is distinct from public navigation camera defects; both remain visible in evidence.

## Three visible flaws per bookmark

### rain-flat

- The pale card merges with its ground.
- The object occupies little of the desktop scene.
- Fold lines and sheet edges are not tactile.

### rain-half-fold

- Wall hinge is a uniform cylinder.
- Low-contrast sheets flatten the fold hierarchy.
- The surrounding card dominates the useful mechanism.

### rain-open

- Slats and card have little material separation.
- Roof has uniform colour/roughness.
- Joinery lacks close-view construction detail.

### rain-inhabit

- Seat and paper figure read as proxies.
- Pins have no visible seats/caps/fasteners.
- Card texture and panel edges lack close detail.

### roof-grazing

- Roof/hinge is cropped in portrait inspection.
- Surface fibre is barely perceptible.
- White slats merge with the ground.

### rain-plan

- Closed roof obscures the architectural interior by design.
- Board grid competes with roof seams.
- No material depth is visible from this view.

### rain-section

- Clipped reader becomes an unhelpful sliver.
- Ground/horizon forms large flat tonal bands.
- Clipping has no deliberate cut-edge treatment.

### vellum-low-sun

- Vellum resembles a pale solid panel at this distance.
- Long shadow has little contact depth.
- Paper and card roughness are nearly uniform.

### shelter-rain

- Rain is thin uniform line geometry.
- Green seat dots read as diagnostics.
- Largest roof remains uniform and thin.

### listening-pavilion

- Bench top nearly disappears into card.
- Frames and sails have simplified connections.
- Paper surface lacks tactile variation.

### sunset-theatre

- Steps merge into the card.
- Wing panels repeat with little physical edge detail.
- Strongly geometric shadows out-read the pale forms.

### postcard-return

- Flat card has only 5/255 robust luminance contrast.
- Large grey field overwhelms fine printing.
- Scaled-down fixture details do not read.
